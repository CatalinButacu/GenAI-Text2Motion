## Task 1: Reliability and Academic Rigor

*Standards applied:* Gebru et al. (2021, *Datasheets for Datasets*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010); Kapoor & Narayanan (2023, *Leakage and the Reproducibility Crisis in ML-Based Science*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.07048) for capture-disjoint splits; Artstein & Poesio (2008, *Computational Linguistics*, https://aclanthology.org/J08-4004/) requiring inter-annotator agreement (IAA, chance-corrected label concordance) $\ge 0.80$; Hubert & Arabie (1985, *J. Classification*) for Adjusted Rand Index (ARI, chance-corrected pairwise partition agreement).

### 1. Training Data Reliability — Verdict: Standard for benchmark parity, flawed as a clean generalization test (*Confidence: High*)
- **Cross-split frame leakage (near-duplicate/overlapping clips):** HumanML3D splits clip IDs rather than source AMASS recordings (`data/HumanML3D_official/index.csv`). Example: `MPI_mosh/50007/stretches_poses.npy` spans all three splits—frames `8..208` in `train.txt:6152` (`007652`, `index.csv:7654`), `101..301` in `test.txt:1205` (`010226`, `index.csv:10228`, **107 frames shared with train**), and `217..417` in `val.txt:2` (`012808`, `index.csv:12810`). Similarly, `val.txt:54` (`000645`, frames `124..324`, `index.csv:647`) overlaps 163/200 frames ($81.5\%$) with `train.txt:5229,3184` (`006505`, `003913`), and `test.txt:1` (`004822`, `index.csv:4824`) overlaps 86 frames with `train.txt:297` (`000371`, `index.csv:373`).
- **Mirrored (`M`) clips and test population mismatch:** `base_clip_ids` (`src/text2motion/motion/storage.py:39-46`) strips `M` prefixes per split, keeping mirrors out of `val`/`test` (`src/text2motion/motion/datasets.py:56`), which prevents intra-split mirror leaks into eval. However, excluding `M` clips on `test` deviates from Guo et al.'s official 4,648-sample evaluation population (`doc/notes/lessons/04a-the-data-pipeline.md:190-192`, `src/text2motion/evaluation/nn_matcher.py:17`).
- **Caption label noise and POS-tag corruption:** Crowd captions contain left/right reversals on the same clip (`data/HumanML3D_official/texts/000002.txt:1-4`, `000005.txt:1-4`), filename-reading confessions instead of visual description (`texts/000011.txt:2` vs wrong wave/kick action in `000011.txt:1`), spelling noise (`swinge` in 359 train clips, `doc/notes/verb_inventory.csv:34`), and severe spaCy POS errors (`left` tagged `VERB` in 1,044 train clips, `side` in 409, `verb_inventory.csv:11,31`). CLIP reads raw text, isolating baseline training from POS noise (`.claude/docs/STATUS.md:10`), but `M`-caption keyword swapping corrupts POS tags (`left/VERB` in `texts/M005283.txt:2`).
- **Caption time ranges:** Only $3.6\%$ ($1{,}274 / 34{,}948$) of train captions carry non-zero `#start#end` (`STATUS.md:23`). `parse_text_file` (`storage.py:165-171`) coerces `nan` endpoints (`texts/000007.txt:2` `#nan#0.0`, `000013.txt:2` `#nan#nan`, 192 files) to `0.0` (whole-clip). Sub-2.0 s ranges (`#0.0#1.0` in 86 base files, e.g., `texts/001078.txt:1`) fall below `min_motion_len = 40` frames ($2.0\text{ s}$ at $20\text{ fps}$) and are dropped (`datasets.py:167-172`, `src/text2motion/evaluation/evaluator.py:85`).
- **Published HumanML3D critiques:** (1) *TMR* (Petrovich et al., ICCV 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.00976) proves R-precision in pools of 32 suffers from false negatives due to near-duplicate captions across clips (`STATUS.md:10` notes 27 exact duplicates; our `verb_generation_probe` confirms Guo's matcher retrieves the ground-truth verb in top-5 only $31.5\%$ of the time on real clips, `STATUS.md:11`). (2) Meng et al. (2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.16575) and Fan et al. (*MotionMillion*, ICCV 2025, https://arxiv.org/html/2507.07095v1, citing `EricGuo5513/HumanML3D#26`) show the 6D inverse-kinematics rotation block in 263-dim features contains twist errors. (3) *Motion-X* (Lin et al., NeurIPS 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.00818) critiques lab-only indoor bias and missing hands/face. (4) This repo proved upstream `AMASS_263` has wrong frame-rate clocks on KIT/EKUT ($16.7\text{ fps}$) and DFaust/TotalCapture ($10\text{ fps}$, `doc/notes/amass_timing_audit.csv:6-15`).

### 2. Derived Label Layer (186 Action Groups) — Verdict: Reject as a validated taxonomy; acceptable only as exploratory stratification (*Confidence: High*)
- **Exact IAA counts (computed by reading `verb_labels_data.csv:2-441`, `verb_labels_eval.csv:2-443`, `verb_vocabulary.csv:2-443`):**
  - *Counting method & library:* `scikit-learn` computes Cohen's $\kappa$ (`sklearn.metrics.cohen_kappa_score`) and ARI (`sklearn.metrics.adjusted_rand_score`). `verb_labels_eval.csv` labels all $442$ verbs ($\ge 5$ train clips); `verb_labels_data.csv` has only $440$ rows, omitting `bump` and `row` (`verb_labels_eval.csv:322,380`), which `scripts/qa/action_vocabulary.py:51-52,68-70` masked inside `DECISIONS`.
  - *Status Cohen's $\kappa$:* Across the $N=440$ shared verbs, row totals (`data`) are $(336\text{ action}, 63\text{ not\_action}, 32\text{ tagger\_error}, 9\text{ typo})$ and column totals (`eval`) are $(325, 75, 29, 11)$. Diagonal counts are $(314, 55, 27, 9) = 405$ agreements; the $35$ disagreements are $20$ `(action, not_action)`, $8$ `(not_action, action)`, $3$ `(tagger_error, action)`, $2$ `(action, tagger_error)`, and $2$ `(tagger_error, typo)`. With $p_o = \frac{405}{440} = 0.9205$ and $p_e = \frac{336\cdot 325 + 63\cdot 75 + 32\cdot 29 + 9\cdot 11}{440^2} = \frac{114{,}952}{193{,}600} = 0.5938$:
    $$\kappa_{440} = \frac{p_o - p_e}{1 - p_e} = \frac{63{,}248}{78{,}648} = 0.804 \quad (\kappa_{442} = \frac{63{,}386}{79{,}740} = 0.795 \text{ counting the 2 omissions as a 5th class}).$$
  - *Group ARI:* Restricting to the $n = 323$ mutually grouped verbs (`status` $\in \{\text{action},\text{typo}\}$ in both; $228$ `agree` + $95$ `split`), total pairs $P = \binom{323}{2} = 52{,}003$. Summing $\binom{s}{2}$ over all multi-member clusters gives $A = \sum_i \binom{a_i}{2} = 553$ pairs in `data` (40 multi-member clusters, max size 19 for `walk`), $B = \sum_j \binom{b_j}{2} = 271$ pairs in `eval` (39 multi-member clusters, max size 11 for `go`), and $M = \sum_{i,j} \binom{n_{ij}}{2} = 136\text{ (27 agreed groups)} + 66\text{ (8 shared split cells: } \binom{9}{2}+\binom{6}{2}+\binom{5}{2}+5\binom{2}{2}\text{)} = 202$ co-clustered pairs. With $E = \frac{553 \times 271}{52{,}003} = 2.8818$:
    $$\mathrm{ARI} = \frac{202 - 2.8818}{\frac{1}{2}(553 + 271) - 2.8818} = \frac{10{,}354{,}743}{21{,}275{,}373} = 0.487.$$
    `data` merges over twice as many verb pairs as `eval` ($553$ vs $271$). Also, raw string comparison (`action_vocabulary.py:76`) falsely flags identical clusters with different head names—e.g., `{lay, lie}` (`lie` vs `lay`, `verb_labels_data.csv:117,172`, `verb_labels_eval.csv:117,172`)—as `split`.
- **Audit of tie-break, `DECISIONS`, and failed motion check:**
  - *Unblinded overrides:* `DECISIONS` (`action_vocabulary.py:20-58`) unilaterally overrides 37 verbs without a blinded adjudicator, and `doc/notes/verb_labels_tiebreak.csv:96` (`smack,1,orchestrator decision: open-hand strike`) mixes orchestrator edits into the third-list file.
  - *Code defects in `named_groups`:* `DECISIONS` marks `perform` as `not_action` (`action_vocabulary.py:23`), leaving its typo `preform` (`verb_vocabulary.csv:404`) orphaned as a standalone action group `preform` (`doc/notes/action_groups.csv:21`), while `singe` (`typo` for `sing`, `<5` clips) becomes group `singe` (`action_groups.csv:2`), contradicting the vocalization exclusion of `talk/say/shout/speak/cry/laugh` (`action_vocabulary.py:30-34,50-51`).
  - *Ignored falsification gate:* `scripts/qa/action_group_motion_check.py:18,110-113` pre-specified `MIN_POSITIVE = 0.80`; positive controls scored $49/88 = 55.7\%$ (`doc/notes/action_group_merges.csv`, with $8/88 = 9.1\%$ random false positives), triggering `"test NOT VALID: keep the disputed verbs apart"`. Yet `verb_labels_tiebreak.csv` kept only $6/95$ disputed verbs apart (`choice=0`) and merged $89/95$ ($57$ to list 1, $32$ to list 2).
- **Permissible vs. forbidden thesis claims:**
  - *May claim:* Descriptive error breakdown across the **22 calibrated, matcher-measurable ungrouped verbs** (`STATUS.md:30,42`, `doc/notes/verb_calibration.csv`, $z < 2$, $\ge 20$ clips/split), and qualitative/exploratory long-tail grouping sensitivity if reported alongside ungrouped lemma results.

## Assumptions and Falsifying Test

### Assumptions
1. MotionMillion and Motion-X++ contain $\ge 15$ clips per rare verb (`juggle`, `row`, `skate`, `ride`, `yawn`) via their HAA500/IDEA400/MotionGV video subsets, inferred from HAA500's 20-clip-per-class design and dataset cards without downloading gated archives (*Confidence: Medium*).
2. Monocular video pseudo-GT subsets (`MotionGV`, `IDEA400`, `HAA500`) after $30\to 20\text{ fps}$ conversion exceed the $2.0\times$ ($102.9\text{ mm}$) frozen-FSQ MPJPE gate, inferred from AIST++ ($188.7\text{ mm}$) and 100STYLE ($382.5\text{ mm}$) failing the same gate (*Confidence: High*).
3. `verb_labels_data.csv` and `verb_labels_eval.csv` were produced independently prior to `DECISIONS` adjudication (*Confidence: Medium*).

  1. Partition `test.txt` using `data/HumanML3D_official/index.csv` into clips whose `source_path` appears in `train.txt` vs. capture-disjoint `test` clips, and score the frozen `step50` twins on both subsets: if R@1 differs by $<0.01$ and FID (at matched sample count $N$) differs by $<0.05$, cross-split capture leakage does not materially inflate the thesis headline numbers.
